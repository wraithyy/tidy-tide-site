# Tidy Tide - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

Tidy Tide ("the game") is developed by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data is
processed when you play.

## Data the game itself stores

The game does not have accounts and does not send your progress to our own servers. Your progress, settings,
stars, coins and reef decorations are stored on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To understand where players get stuck and to improve the levels, the game uses Google Firebase Analytics.
When you have allowed it, Google may collect and process:

- gameplay events: which level you started, finished, failed or left, how many moves and seconds it took,
  whether you used undo or hints, what you chose after running out of moves, and whether you watched a
  rewarded video or removed ads,
- a random app instance ID (not linked to your name or e-mail), device information (model, operating system,
  language, approximate location derived from the IP address) and the length of your sessions.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and
Switzerland), the game collects analytics only after you agree in the consent dialog shown at first launch.
If you refuse, no analytics data is collected. You can change your choice at any time in
**Settings > Privacy settings**. Elsewhere analytics are on by default.

Google acts as a processor for this data. See the
[Firebase privacy information](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy) and the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset the app instance ID by
clearing the game's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

- the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA),
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device information (model, operating system, language),
- information about ad interactions (views, clicks),
- app-instance identifiers and crash or diagnostic data from the ad SDK.

How Google uses this data is described in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent
before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy settings**.
If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## In-app purchase

"Remove ads" is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the game only receives a confirmation that
the purchase was made, never your payment details.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.
