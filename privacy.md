# Tidy Tide - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-01

Tidy Tide ("the game") is developed by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what data is
processed when you play.

## Data the game itself stores

The game does not have accounts and does not send your data to our servers. Your progress, settings, stars,
coins and reef decorations are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

- the advertising ID of your device (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA),
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
- device information (model, operating system, language),
- information about ad interactions (views, clicks).

How Google uses this data is described in
[How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent
before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in **Settings > Privacy settings**.
If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

## Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available at this address.
